# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome differing experience levels and backgrounds. Discuss ideas and evidence, acknowledge mistakes, and give constructive feedback without personal attacks.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content directed at participants, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and project-related interactions.

Report concerns to the maintainer, Samuel (kmab), using the [kmab feedback form](https://kmab5.github.io/kmab-feedback/?product=singalong&type=other). State that it is a conduct report and share only the necessary details. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues. Maintainers will review concerns discreetly, consider the context, and may remove content, warn participants, restrict participation or ban repeated/severe violations. Affected participants may request a review through the same channel.

Maintainers follow the same standards. Enforcement should be proportionate, consistent and focused on protecting participation and privacy.
