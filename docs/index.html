Android 12+Release notes ↗

The latest signed universal APK, directly from GitHub.

Other APK downloads

The main download is universal. Choose a smaller APK if you know your device’s architecture.

    1. 01

      Install Singalong.

      Open the downloaded APK. Allow installation from your browser or file manager if Android asks. Same-key updates keep your library.

    2. 02

      Connect your music.

      Choose your players and enable notification access so Singalong can read song metadata and playback position. Add the Singalong tile through Quick Settings → Edit to turn tracking on or off from the shade.

    3. 03

      Bring up the words.

      Allow display over other apps, play a song and open floating lyrics. Find the view that feels like you.

    Read the privacy details ↗
    Where do the lyrics come from?

    LRCLIB supplies timed and plain lyrics, with lyrics.ovh as a plain-text fallback. Coverage varies. Source attribution stays with saved versions.

    Does every song follow automatically?

    Following needs timestamps and playback position from your player. Word glow uses supplied word timing when present; line-only timing can use estimated word progression. Untimed lyrics have optional experimental sync that you can turn off.

    What goes online?

    Song search details go to lyric providers, never audio. Optional English subtext sends opened lyrics to MyMemory, which stores submitted text. Google Translate is a separate external action. ML Kit identifies language locally but may send SDK metrics. Cards and album palettes render on your phone. This website has no analytics.

    Will it work with my player?

    Singalong uses Android media sessions. Compatibility depends on the metadata and playback position exposed by your player. Some Android screens intentionally hide overlays; Singalong respects those restrictions.

    Something didn’t feel right?

    Send the app version, player and steps through kmab feedback. If the app offers a crash report, review it before sharing. Reports are never uploaded automatically.
