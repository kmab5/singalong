Clear about what it does

Your voice.
Your business.

No microphone recording. Singalong uses player metadata and playback position. Your library stays on your phone.

Where do the words come from?

LRCLIB supplies timed and plain lyrics, with lyrics.ovh as a plain-text fallback. Coverage varies. Source attribution stays with every saved version.

What goes online?

Song titles and artists are sent to lyric providers. If you enable English subtext, opened lyric text is sent to MyMemory, which stores submitted text. Language detection happens on your phone. This website has no analytics.

Does every song follow automatically?

No. Automatic following needs timestamps and playback position from the music player. Untimed versions can use the optional estimated mode, or you can scroll manually. You can turn following off.

Something didn’t work?

This is a personal app in active development. Use About → Send feedback in the app, or send feedback here. If a crash was recorded, About lets you save its details and attach them yourself.
