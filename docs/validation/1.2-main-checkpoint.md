## Album-art palette

Album art is an additional choice beside the ten curated palettes. A bounded grid samples at most 4,096 pixels of the current draft cover off the UI thread. Population-based quantization selects a predominant color and, where available, a second prevalent color. A dark tinted base, light text and contrasting accent retain the color direction while keeping the card readable. Mostly transparent images without usable pixels do not invent a palette. No network request or dependency was added.

The immutable decoded-image state carries its colors into swatches, preview and export. A new cover refreshes them; hiding the foreground cover or choosing a separate Photo background does not change their source. Missing artwork disables the option with an explanation. All previously implemented card controls and original wording remain intact.
